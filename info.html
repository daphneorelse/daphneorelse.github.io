rmory is a workshop, dedicated to creating dangerously powerful software for your music & audio production. What you get are musical weapons—referencing everything from nuclear detonators to chainsaws, and enabling all kinds of cutting-edge experimentation.

This operation is just me, Daphne :)

I first became obsessed with music software when I was 15, and over ten years later my obsession is only growing. I studied at CCRMA (Center for Computer Reasearch in Music & Acoustics) at Stanford and earned a degree in Human Computer Interaction. I love making the software, and I love using it for my own music even more.

A big belief of mine is that the look and feel of instruments influence our music more than we even know. Much of my efforts is dedicated to storytelling using software, which I hope inspires users to try things in their music that might scare them. In fact, I hope it scares them.
